MODEL REFERENCE

What this simulation represents

This is a controller development model for the MANTA surface craft. It predicts planar movement well enough to compare steering logic and disturbance rejection. It is not a CFD result or a validated prediction of the final craft.

Physics included

  • Surge, sway and yaw velocity with mass and yaw inertia.
  • Independent, fixed-direction port and starboard thrust, including reverse.
  • Differential-thrust yaw moment from the distance between the motors.
  • Speed-dependent lateral keel force and steering moment.
  • Linear and quadratic water-relative drag.
  • A steady current vector, periodic horizontal wave forcing, yaw disturbance and temporary gusts.

Controller included

A line-of-sight waypoint follower selects a target ahead of the nearest point on the current route segment. A heading PID produces a signed steering demand. The keel-share setting allocates that demand between keel angle and differential motor thrust. A separate speed controller reduces thrust near a waypoint and stops at the final one.

Current limits

  • No hull-water CFD, buoyancy, planing, roll, pitch, heave, propeller wash or wave elevation.
  • Drag, keel effectiveness, motor thrust and mass have not been measured.
  • Controller gains that work here must still be retuned during tank and open-water testing.
  • The visual model is a reduced browser mesh derived from P.O.C. Quick Draft v1/v1.obj. Its geometry does not drive hydrodynamic coefficients.

Recommended validation tests

  1. Measure total craft mass and centre of mass.
  2. Measure static thrust from each motor at several commands.
  3. Run straight-line coast-down tests to estimate longitudinal drag.
  4. Command constant motor differences and keel angles to measure turn rate.
  5. Replace the estimated values, then tune the controller from low gains upward.
